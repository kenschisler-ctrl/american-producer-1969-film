Converging
Three ships working the same stretch of the Embarcadero: the Afoundria inbound from the Gate for Oakland, the M M Dant up from Anchorage 9, swinging a full circle to come in to Pier 15-17, and the American Producer, down from Port Chicago, bound for Anchorage 9 for bunkers.

What could have been
A full load of bombs, bound for Vietnam, against a wooden pier.
The hazard map: total destruction for a quarter mile around the pier end — the neighbouring piers and the foot of Telegraph Hill. Chinatown, North Beach and the rest of the hill in the heavy zone. The Bay Bridge itself in the moderate zone, effects reaching Oakland and Berkeley.
Port Chicago, 1944: 4,600 tons of munitions, thirty-five miles away, shattered windows across the Bay Area and killed 320 men.

Aftermath
The fire department foamed the dock against sparks. A tug put a towline on her stern and pulled her off. She was escorted back to Port Chicago to discharge.

SS American Producer — declared a total loss.

Sold for scrapping to Mitsui & Co., May 22, 1969.

Scrapped at Kaohsiung, Taiwan, July 1969.

She had earned six battle stars in the Second World War.
