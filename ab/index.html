Clip 4 — two takes

The same opening still and the same direction, rendered by two different models. Both are the first half of clip 4 (10 seconds): the near miss pushed out into the bay, waterfront small at the left, the Afoundria crossing behind.

Two things to weigh beyond the picture. Omni maxes out at 10 seconds, so a 20-second shot has to be two joined halves — which is how clip 4 is already built. And it only renders 16:9, not the film's 21:9, so using it would mean cropping the top and bottom off every frame or changing the film's shape. H3 gives 21:9 natively and runs to 15 seconds.
