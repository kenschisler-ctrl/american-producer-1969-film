American Producer · March 5, 1969

Clip Review

The film is made of ten clips, numbered here in the order they play. To comment on one, just say its number — for example, “Clip 4: the Dant should turn sooner.”

Updated October 25, 2026. Clips 4, 5 and 6 are new, rebuilt from your notes and drawing. Clips 7–10 are still the October 17 versions and are next to be redone. The clips play silent and without the film’s titles and music. The whole film on the main page has not been reassembled yet.

1

Dawn

15 seconds · starts at 0:00
Unchanged — no notes

San Francisco Bay before sunrise, fog on the water, the city lights still on. The title card “March 5, 1969” appears over this clip.

2

Port Chicago

15 seconds · starts at 0:15
Unchanged — no notes

The American Producer at the Port Chicago pier the day before, being loaded with bombs for Da Nang.

3

The Bay from the Bridge

18 seconds · starts at 0:30
Unchanged — no notes

A slow look around from the Bay Bridge: Coit Tower and the piers, the Golden Gate, Alcatraz, and the open water north where the Producer came down from.

4

Head-On

15 seconds · starts at 0:48
New · October 25 · final

From high over the bay off the Bay Bridge. The Dant (grey) is heading away up the bay and the American Producer (black) comes down toward her, almost head-on — a near miss. Then the Dant turns left and the Producer turns right, toward the land, and they end side by side heading for the piers, the Producer on the far side, her port to the Dant’s starboard. Built from Ken’s drawing.

5

Between Them

7 seconds · starts at 1:03
New · October 25 · final

Low ahead of both ships as they come in bow-on against the sunrise, nearly touching. The drone rises steeply and turns over them, ending looking straight down on the two ships side by side. (Cut before the pier comes into view.)

6

Coming In

15 seconds · starts at 1:10
New · October 25 · for review

Picks up from above the two ships. The drone rises to reveal the waterfront and the 1969 skyline — downtown and the new Bank of America tower, the Ferry Building, Telegraph Hill and Coit Tower. The Dant swings away to the left, back toward the Bay Bridge, and the Producer carries on alone, square on toward the end of the wedge-shaped Pier 27-29.

7

Allision

From a small boat beside the pier: her bow strikes the end of Pier 27-29.

8

What Could Have Been

October 17 version · to redo from your notes

High above the waterfront. In the film, rings drawn over this clip show how far the damage would have reached if her cargo had gone off.

9

The Grey Ship

9 seconds · starts at 1:49
October 17 version · to redo from your notes

The Manderson Victory docking just west of the Bay Bridge — the ship Ken’s father was piloting that morning.

10

Aftermath

12 seconds · starts at 1:58
October 17 version · to redo from your notes

The dock foamed against sparks, and a tug on her stern pulling her off the pier — the tug Ken’s father boarded.
